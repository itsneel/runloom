# Platform administration

Platform roles are separate from organization and team administration.

## Use the console

Sign in with a platform account and open `/platform` on your Studio origin. Platform-only staff go directly to the console after login.

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Platform staff | View platform identity, service health and organization metadata |
| Platform administrator | Also manage staff access and view platform audit events |

Neither role automatically grants access to tenant workflows or their data. An organization administrator is not a platform administrator.

## Local versus hosted

Local setup creates separate platform admin/staff accounts and prints their defaults. Existing passwords and platform access are preserved.

Never use public local passwords for hosted deployments. Hosted access also requires the configured authentication strength and session-age checks. Expand the reference for provisioning and restrictions.
